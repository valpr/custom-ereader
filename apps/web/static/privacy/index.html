Valpr Reader Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026 • Effective Date: September 8, 2026

Summary of Core Privacy Principles

  • Local-First: Valpr Reader runs entirely in your web browser. All book processing and reading statistics are stored locally on your device.
  • No Proprietary Servers: Valpr Reader operates no backend application servers, databases, or analytics services. We never collect, track, or sell your personal data.
  • Direct Cloud Connections: When optional Google Drive sync is enabled, your browser communicates directly with Google APIs via encrypted HTTPS.

1. Overview of Valpr Reader

Valpr Reader is a free, open-source, web-based e-book reader designed specifically for Japanese language learning and immersion (supporting EPUB, HTMLZ, and TXT formats), Yomitan dictionary popups, and reading statistics.

This Privacy Policy explains how Valpr Reader handles your information when you access or use the application at https://valpr.github.io/ or https://valpr.github.io/reader, or self-host your own instance.

2. Information We Process & How It Is Handled

Valpr Reader processes the following types of information strictly to provide reader functionality:

  • Book & Library Data: E-book files and cover images imported by you are stored in your browser's IndexedDB. They never leave your device unless you choose to sync them to your personal cloud storage.
  • Reading Progress & Statistics: Reading positions, bookmarks, character counts, reading speed, and session times are calculated and stored locally in your browser.
  • Reader Preferences: Themes, fonts, layouts, and reading settings are stored locally in your browser's LocalStorage and IndexedDB.
  • Cloud Authentication Tokens: When you connect Google Drive or OneDrive, OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally in your browser and may optionally be encrypted with a password.

3. Google API Services & Google Drive User Data

Valpr Reader offers an optional synchronization feature with Google Drive. Connecting Google Drive is completely optional; the application is fully functional offline without signing in to Google.

A. Requested Google OAuth Scopes

When you choose to connect Google Drive, Valpr Reader requests the following scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

Per Google's official scope definition, this scope only grants access to files and folders that were created by or opened with Valpr Reader.

B. What Google Data We Access

  • Valpr Reader only reads, creates, and updates files inside the dedicated application folder (named valpr-reader-data) created by the app in your Google Drive.
  • These files include synced reading progress, bookmarks, reading statistics, user settings/profiles, and optional book archives uploaded by you.
  • What We Do NOT Access: Valpr Reader cannot access, view, modify, or delete any other files, documents, photos, spreadsheets, emails, or personal information in your Google Drive or Google Account. We do not request or store your Google profile, contact lists, or personal identity information.

C. How Google Data Is Handled and Transferred

  • All communication with Google APIs occurs directly from your browser to Google servers (https://accounts.google.com, https://oauth2.googleapis.com, and https://www.googleapis.com) via secure Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • No intermediary server is used. Data is never routed through, processed by, or cached on any server operated by Valpr Reader.
  • Google user data is never used for advertising, marketing, analytics, or profiling.
  • Google user data is never sold, rented, or transferred to third parties.

"Valpr Reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements."

4. Microsoft OneDrive Integration

Valpr Reader also optionally supports synchronizing reading progress with Microsoft OneDrive using the Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder and User.Read scopes. Access is strictly confined to Valpr Reader's sandboxed application folder on OneDrive.

5. Data Security & Encryption

All reading data is stored within your browser's origin sandbox (IndexedDB, LocalStorage). All API communications with Google and Microsoft use HTTPS/TLS encryption. You may also configure Valpr Reader with a master password to encrypt OAuth tokens and profiles using AES encryption before persisting them.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

You have complete control over your data at all times:

1. Delete Books & Reading Data In-App

In the Book Manager, select books and click the Delete icon to permanently remove books, reading progress, and bookmarks. In Settings > Storage, you can delete statistics or clear all local database storage.

2. Delete Synced Files in Google Drive

Open Google Drive and delete the valpr-reader-data folder to permanently delete all synced data from Google servers.

3. Disconnect & Revoke Google Access

Disconnect Google Drive at any time under Settings > Storage Sources in Valpr Reader, or immediately revoke access at Google Account Security Permissions.

4. Browser Storage Deletion

Clearing your browser's site data for the application's domain will immediately erase all local state, cached books, and stored tokens.
